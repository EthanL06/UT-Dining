# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 27, 2025  
**Last Updated:** November 27, 2026

**UT Dining** (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information when you use UT Dining.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We do **not** collect any personally identifiable information (such as name, email, or phone number).

However, we may collect certain non-personal, technical information automatically to improve the app’s performance and user experience:

- **Location Data** (with your permission): Used to show your location on the campus map and, if you turn on Nearby Menus, to notify you when you arrive at an open dining hall. For Nearby Menus, iOS watches a small area around each dining hall on your phone and tells the app when you enter one; the app then decides on your phone whether to show a notification. Your location is never sent to our servers or to anyone else.
- **Device & Usage Data**: We use PostHog to anonymously track general app usage patterns, crashes, and performance issues. This includes which screens you open and what you tap. This helps us improve the app but does not personally identify you.
- **Push Notification Token** (only if you allow notifications): If you turn on notifications, the app sends a push token and a random device ID to our servers so we can send you notifications. The device ID is generated by the app and is not tied to your Apple or Google account. If a token stops working, we delete it.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services in our app:

- **Supabase** – For backend functionality like content and data delivery
- **PostHog** – For anonymous usage analytics, crash reports, and performance tracking
- **Expo Push Notifications** (with Apple Push Notification service) – To deliver push notifications

We do **not** share any user data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

---

## 3. Data Storage

Your favorites, meal plan, and settings are stored **locally on your device**. They are never sent to our servers.

- Local data is retained only for as long as the app remains installed.
- If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed.
- The only data we store on our servers is the push notification token and random device ID described above. You can stop push notifications at any time in your device settings. To have your token deleted, contact us at the address below.
- There are no accounts, so there is no other cloud-based data to delete or manage.

---

## 4. Permissions We Request

The app may request the following permissions:

- **Location Access** – To show your current location on the interactive campus map
- **Location Access “Always”** (only if you turn on Nearby Menus) – So your phone can notify you when you arrive at an open dining hall, even when the app is closed. You can turn Nearby Menus off in the app’s Settings at any time.
- **Notifications** – To alert you about dining hall closures, special hours, or new menu updates

These permissions are optional and can be managed through your device settings.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

UT Dining is intended for general use and is **not designed for children under the age of 13**. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children.

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. If we make significant changes, we’ll notify you in the app or through an app update.
